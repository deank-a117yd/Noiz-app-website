## Logo & Alternatives

The Noiz logo mark is a bold, angular yellow icon — a stylized shape resembling a dynamic "N" or sound-wave form — always rendered in **#FDDA5C yellow** on a **deep purple rounded-rectangle** background.

Three approved logo configurations:

| Variant | Description | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Icon Only** | Yellow mark on purple rounded square | App icon, favicon, small placements |
| **Horizontal Lockup** | Icon left + "NOIZ" wordmark right, wide rounded rect | Header, banners, wide contexts |
| **Stacked Lockup** | Icon above "NOIZ" wordmark, square format | Social, print, square contexts |

**Wordmark styling:** "NOIZ" appears in **Archivo Black**, all caps, in **#FDDA5C yellow**.  
**Logo background:** Deep purple, always with rounded corners.  
**No mixing** of logo on non-purple backgrounds unless the moodboard explicitly shows it (one cyan/light version appears in the moodboard — treat as an exception).

## Typography

Single font family across all uses: **Archivo** (Google Fonts).

| Weight | Role |
|---|---|
| **Archivo Black** | Headlines, hero text, primary labels |
| **Archivo Semibold** | Subheadings, emphasis, buttons |
| **Archivo Regular** | Body copy, captions, secondary text |

**Rules:**
- Do not introduce a second typeface.
- Headlines should feel heavy and loud — Archivo Black is the dominant voice.
- All-caps is appropriate for short labels and section headers (matches the brand guide's own formatting).

## Colors

### Primary Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Purple** | `#38155C` | Primary background, logo background, dominant surface color |
| **Electric Yellow / Gold** | `#FDDA5C` | Logo mark, wordmark, primary accent, CTA highlights |
| **Gradient** | `#38155C` → darker/lighter purple | Background fills, hero sections, depth/atmosphere |

### Color Relationships
- **Purple is the canvas. Yellow is the signal.**
- The yellow should punch — use it for the most important element on any given surface (headline, button, logo).
- The gradient gives depth; avoid flat solid purple where a gradient reads more atmospheric.
- White is used sparingly for body text on dark backgrounds (inferred from moodboard typography).

### What the Gradient Looks Like
Based on the moodboard: a deep dark purple-to-purple gradient, ranging from near-black navy at edges to the core `#38155C` purple. It reads cinematic and nocturnal — not bright or neon.

## Moodboard & Visual Aesthetic

### Tagline Seen in Moodboard
> **"THE SOCIAL DIARY FOR LIVE MUSIC."**

This phrase appears on a subway/out-of-home poster mockup — it's a strong secondary positioning line, more descriptive than the 3-word tagline. Both should be in the toolkit:
- **"Rate. Connect. Discover."** — punchy homepage hero
- **"The Social Diary for Live Dance Music."** — deeper descriptor, sub-headlines, about copy

### Aesthetic Direction
The moodboard communicates:
- **Dark & cinematic** — concert lighting, purple-washed environments, nightlife energy
- **Urban & street-level** — subway ads, people in the city, real-world placement
- **Music-first** — mixing boards, DJ setups, live performance contexts
- **App-forward** — phone mockups are prominent; this is a product brand, not just a vibe brand

### Mood Keywords
`nocturnal` `electric` `underground` `live` `social` `raw` `bold` `urban`

## Voice & Tone (Inferred from Brand + Site)

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| **Confident** | Short, declarative statements. No filler. |
| **Community-first** | "For everyone discovering live music" — not just for you, but your scene |
| **Energetic but not hype** | The brand is bold, not loud. Yellow pops, it doesn't shout. |
| **Present tense** | Active voice, action-oriented copy |

---

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Use Archivo Black for all primary headlines
- Lead with yellow as the accent on dark purple backgrounds
- Use the gradient to add atmosphere to hero/full-bleed sections
- Keep logo on rounded purple backgrounds
- Reference live music as an *experience* and *emotion*, not just an event

**Don't:**
- Use the logo on white or light backgrounds (no approved version exists)
- Introduce other font families
- Use yellow as a background color — it's an accent, not a field
- Use flat/bright purple without the gradient in hero contexts
- Write long copy — the brand voice is concise
