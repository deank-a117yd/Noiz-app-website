About

NOIZ is a platform built for discovering and experiencing live music in a more meaningful way. Users can rate and log the live music events they attend, capturing the vibe of each night and building a personal record of their experiences. NOIZ also makes it easy to connect with friends, share ratings, and find the best shows in your area. NOIZ is about capturing and remembering the energy of live music and helping people discover the scenes shaping the next wave of sound.
